# Axis Bridge — Official Corporate Website

> **Connecting Markets. Creating Opportunities. Building Partnerships.**

Axis Bridge is an international cross-border advisory, trade facilitation, and strategic investment firm operating a dedicated bilateral corridor between **Nigeria (West Africa)** and **Egypt (North Africa / MENA)**.

## Overview

- **Bilateral Corridors:** Abuja / Lagos &larr;&rarr; Cairo
- **Core Sectors:** Higher Education & Academic Pathways, Specialized Healthcare & Medical Referrals, Business & Cultural Tourism, Trade & Strategic Investment, Technology Transfer & Systems Integration.
- **Leadership:**
  - **Muhammad Kasim** — Co-Founder & Executive Chairman
  - **Dr. Ahmad Muhammad** — Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer

## Architecture & Technology

- Modern semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3, and vanilla interaction JavaScript.
- Architectural design system aligned with official Axis Bridge branding guidelines:
  - Deep Teal / Sea-Green (`#167C80`)
  - Corporate Midnight Blue (`#0A2540` / `#1A3E63`)
  - Charcoal / Near-Black (`#191D20` / `#252A2E`)
  - Warm Gold (`#B38F0C` / `#D6B030`)
  - Clean Paper (`#FFFFFF` / `#F7F4EC`)
- Interactive horizontal Five Business Pillars progression roadmap.
- Bilateral operating model, governance frameworks, and interactive opportunity intake channels.
